HOPE HOUSE WOMEN'S SHELTER
Emergency shelter services — Collin County

ABOUT US
Hope House has provided emergency shelter since 1998. Our staff includes
licensed case managers and a full-time housing navigator.

WHO WE SERVE
Hope House serves women and women with accompanying children. Guests must
present a valid photo ID at intake and must be residents of Collin County.
Maximum length of stay is 90 days.

WHAT TO BRING
Personal medications, identification, and a change of clothes.
