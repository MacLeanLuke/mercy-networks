RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY KITCHEN — 2026 ANNUAL REPORT

This year Riverside Community Kitchen served 84,000 meals, an increase of
12% over the prior year. Our volunteer base grew to 310 active volunteers
contributing more than 19,000 hours.

We expanded our Tuesday produce distribution and added a second refrigerated
van through a grant from the North Texas Food Coalition.

Thank you to our donors for making this work possible.
