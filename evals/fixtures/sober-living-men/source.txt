NORTHGATE SOBER LIVING

Northgate is a structured sober living residence for adult men in recovery.

ELIGIBILITY
Residents must be men age 18 or older, must have completed a detox or
inpatient treatment program, must remain sober, and must pass a criminal
background check. Residents must show a government-issued ID.

HOUSE RULES
Curfew is 10pm. Weekly house meetings are mandatory.
