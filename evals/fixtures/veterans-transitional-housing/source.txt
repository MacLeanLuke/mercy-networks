VALOR TRANSITIONAL HOUSING PROGRAM

Valor provides transitional housing for veterans working toward permanent
placement. Residents receive case management, employment support, and
benefits navigation.

ELIGIBILITY
Applicants must have served in the United States Armed Forces and be able to
provide a DD-214. Residents must maintain sobriety throughout their stay.
Participation is limited to 24 months.

APPLICATION
Submit an application through the VA housing coordinator.
