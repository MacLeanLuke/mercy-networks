A LETTER FROM OUR DIRECTOR

When Marcy arrived at Bridgeway last spring she had been sleeping in her car
for two months. Like everyone we welcome into the program, she was a woman
over the age of eighteen, and like everyone she agreed to remain sober for
the duration of her stay — that is simply how the house works.

Marcy stayed with us for the full six months our program allows, and left
with a lease in her own name.

We are grateful to everyone who makes Bridgeway possible.
